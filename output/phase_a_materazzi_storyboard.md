**Recommended music track style:** Cinematic tension build — sparse piano/strings intro → builds to orchestral swell. No percussion until the headbutt moment. The structure of the track should mirror the script: quiet → building → sharp hit → swell → resolve.

**Music sources (commercial use only):**
1. **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com → Audio Library) — Free, no attribution required, explicitly cleared for monetized YouTube content. Recommended first choice.

## Production Notes

1. **No match footage anywhere in this storyboard.** Every shot is either a CC-BY licensed photograph or an original generated graphic.
2. **Ken Burns effect can be implemented in:** CapCut, DaVinci Resolve (free), Final Cut Pro, or ffmpeg with the `zoompan` filter.
3. **Text animations** (word-by-word reveal, score flip, ticker): available natively in CapCut and DaVinci Resolve Fusion.
4. **Score flip animation:** DaVinci Resolve Fusion can replicate a departure-board digit flip with a Rotate 3D transform on individual text layers.
5. **Photo colour grading:** A subtle "news/archive" feel on the photos (slight desaturation, navy cast) distinguishes them visually from the graphic sections and signals "real event."
6. **B-roll for the scoring moment (Shot 4):** No licensed photo of Materazzi's actual header exists in Wikimedia Commons. The goal is covered by the score-card graphic instead. This is intentional and avoids copyright risk.
